# 90-second demo script

Before recording: open https://offerguard-api-20f2.onrender.com/health and wait for the answer (wakes the free server). Open the live site in a clean window, zoom 110%.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Show the home page | "Fake job offers cost students money. OfferGuard doesn't give a fraud score. It shows evidence." |
| 0:10 | Click chip 1, Analyze | "Paste any offer. Here is the result." |
| 0:20 | Point at the highlighted message | "Every warning links to the exact words in the message." |
| 0:30 | Scroll to Findings | "Each finding has the claim, the source, the status and what we're unsure about." |
| 0:40 | Click chip 2, Analyze | "A real company, but the email domain, link, registration number and city don't match its record." |
| 0:55 | Show checklist + a Copy message | "It tells the user what to do and writes the verification message for them." |
| 1:05 | Tick "Simulate URL check failure", Analyze | "When a check can't run, we say not checked, never safe." |
| 1:15 | Open the Evaluation tab | "We measured the rules on 20 labelled cases. 90% precision and recall. The 2 failures are shown openly." |
| 1:25 | Back to home | "OfferGuard: evidence, not guesses." |

Recording on Windows: press Win+Alt+R to start and stop (Xbox Game Bar). The file is saved in Videos\Captures. Upload to YouTube as Unlisted, or to Google Drive with "Anyone with the link".
